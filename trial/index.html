How should guests find the India picture?

Four trials. Swiping and tapping the picture work in all of them, plus the small flower button.

1. ArrowsRound arrow buttons on the picture edge, labelled India / Germany.2. SwitchA Germany | India switch under the picture.3. Button on the pictureA small pulsing “See India →” button on the picture.4. Plays by itselfThe picture changes to India and back on its own until someone touches it, with two dots under it.
